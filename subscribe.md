## Subscribe

New posts, by RSS. No email address, no algorithm deciding what you see, and nothing that tracks you — your reader fetches the feed directly from this site.

<div class="feed-url">
  <span class="feed-url-label">Feed URL</span>
  <code>https://www.bitsnbeyond.blog/feed.xml</code>
</div>

### How to use it

1. Open your feed reader and look for **Add feed**, **Subscribe**, or a **+** button.
2. Paste the URL above.
3. New posts show up on their own. Nothing else to do.

Most readers will also find the feed if you simply paste `bitsnbeyond.blog` — this site publishes an autodiscovery link, so the reader can locate it without the exact address.

### Readers worth trying

| Reader | Where it runs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **NetNewsWire** | macOS, iOS | Free and open source |
| **Feedly** | Web, iOS, Android | Free tier is plenty for a few feeds |
| **Inoreader** | Web, iOS, Android | Good filtering if you follow a lot |
| **Reeder** | macOS, iOS | Paid, well designed |

Any of them will do. RSS is an open format, so the feed works the same everywhere and you can move readers without losing your subscriptions.

### A note on the raw feed

If you open [the feed itself](/feed.xml) in a browser you'll usually see a wall of XML, or a download prompt. That's expected — the feed is written for software, not for people. Paste the URL into a reader instead.
