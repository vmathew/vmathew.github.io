## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Vivek Mathew**. I build AI systems that survive contact with a bank's risk committee.

I'm an AI and software engineering leader with 19+ years in distributed systems and cloud platforms, currently a **Principal Architect at Fifth Third Bank**, where I built the bank's governed AI developer platform: Amazon Bedrock with guardrails enforced on every call, model access gated at the organization level, and observability built in from the start. A big part of that work is FinOps: per-team attribution and reporting for AI and cloud spend, so leadership can see where the money goes and set policy against it rather than discovering it on the invoice.

Before that: Senior Cloud Application Architect at **AWS**, and Principal Architect at **Discover Financial Services**, where platforms my teams shipped became two US patents ([US11886278B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11886278B2/en), [US11868749B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11868749B2/en)).

My leadership has been technical so far: architecture, platforms, and the governance structures that shape what hundreds of engineers can safely build. I'm deliberately growing toward leading engineering organizations, and this blog is partly where I work those ideas out in the open.

The full story is on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vjmathew).

### What I write about
- **AI governance**: guardrails, observability, and making AI adoption auditable
- **FinOps**: a proprietary cost control system for leadership, a VS Code extension for Bedrock with token usage and cost tracked on every call, and giving leadership real control over AI and cloud spend
- **Enterprise AI platforms and agents**: Bedrock, AgentCore, MCP, RAG, and what works in production
- **Cloud architecture**: AWS, serverless, event-driven design, Terraform and CDK

*Opinions are my own and don't represent my employer. I write about general patterns, never a specific organization's environment.*
